# XHTML

With the flag `ORG_HTML_FLAG_XHTML` (`--xhtml`), the empty elements are closed
as XML requires.

```````````````````````````````` example
a\\
b
.
<p>a<br />
b</p>
.
-x
````````````````````````````````

```````````````````````````````` example
-----
.
<hr />
.
-x
````````````````````````````````

```````````````````````````````` example
[[file:x.png]]
.
<div class="figure">
<p><img src="x.png" alt="x.png" />
</p>
</div>
.
-x
````````````````````````````````

```````````````````````````````` example
#+BEGIN_VERSE
a
b
#+END_VERSE
.
<p class="verse">a<br />
b</p>
.
-x
````````````````````````````````
