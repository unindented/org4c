# Subscripts and Superscripts

With the flag `ORG_FLAG_SUBSUPERSCRIPTS`, ORG4C recognizes subscripts and
superscripts. (Emacs Org does so by default, but it is often surprising.)

The script follows a non-whitespace character. It is either `*`, a text in
braces, or a word (optionally starting with a sign, and ending with an
alphanumeric character).

```````````````````````````````` example
a_b x^2 H_{2}O e^-1 x^{a_b} a_* a_1.5b, c_x.
.
<p>a<sub>b</sub> x<sup>2</sup> H<sub>2</sub>O e<sup>-1</sup> x<sup>a<sub>b</sub></sup> a<sub>*</sub> a<sub>1.5b</sub>, c<sub>x</sub>.</p>
.
--fsubsuperscripts
````````````````````````````````

```````````````````````````````` example
snake_case_name and _underline_ and x__y
.
<p>snake<sub>case</sub><sub>name</sub> and <span class="underline">underline</span> and x_<sub>y</sub></p>
.
--fsubsuperscripts
````````````````````````````````

```````````````````````````````` example
a _b and a^ b
.
<p>a _b and a^ b</p>
.
--fsubsuperscripts
````````````````````````````````

With the flag `ORG_FLAG_SUBSUPERSCRIPTS_BRACED` (Org's `#+OPTIONS: ^:{}`),
only the scripts in braces are recognized.

```````````````````````````````` example
a_b H_{2}O x^{a_b} e^-1
.
<p>a_b H<sub>2</sub>O x<sup>a_b</sup> e^-1</p>
.
--fsubsuperscripts-braced
````````````````````````````````

Without the flags, they are not recognized.

```````````````````````````````` example
a_b H_{2}O
.
<p>a_b H_{2}O</p>
````````````````````````````````
