# Hard Soft Breaks

With the flag `ORG_HTML_FLAG_HARD_SOFT_BREAKS` (`--fhard-soft-breaks`), all soft
line breaks are rendered as hard ones (as Org's `\n:t`).

```````````````````````````````` example
foo
bar
baz
.
<p>foo<br>
bar<br>
baz</p>
.
--fhard-soft-breaks
````````````````````````````````

This applies also to the paragraphs in list items.

```````````````````````````````` example
- a
  b
.
<ul>
<li>a<br>
b</li>
</ul>
.
--fhard-soft-breaks
````````````````````````````````

Verbatim contents are not affected.

```````````````````````````````` example
#+BEGIN_SRC c
x
y
#+END_SRC
.
<pre><code class="language-c">x
y
</code></pre>
.
--fhard-soft-breaks
````````````````````````````````

The in-buffer `#+OPTIONS:` override the flag (unless `--fignore-options`).

```````````````````````````````` example
#+OPTIONS: \n:nil
foo
bar
.
<p>foo
bar</p>
.
--fhard-soft-breaks
````````````````````````````````
