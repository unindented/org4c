# Tables

A table consists of lines starting with `|`. The cells are separated by
`|`.

```````````````````````````````` example
| a | b |
| c | d |
.
<table>
<tbody>
<tr>
<td>a</td>
<td>b</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td>c</td>
<td>d</td>
</tr>
</tbody>
</table>
````````````````````````````````

The trailing `|` is optional. Missing cells are added.

```````````````````````````````` example
| a | b
| c |
.
<table>
<tbody>
<tr>
<td>a</td>
<td>b</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td>c</td>
<td></td>
</tr>
</tbody>
</table>
````````````````````````````````

The rows before the first rule form the header (if there are other rows
after it).

```````````````````````````````` example
| h1 | h2 |
|----+----|
| a  | b  |
.
<table>
<thead>
<tr>
<th>h1</th>
<th>h2</th>
</tr>
</thead>
<tbody>
<tr>
<td>a</td>
<td>b</td>
</tr>
</tbody>
</table>
````````````````````````````````

```````````````````````````````` example
| a | b |
|---+---|
.
<table>
<tbody>
<tr>
<td>a</td>
<td>b</td>
</tr>
</tbody>
</table>
````````````````````````````````

Other rules separate the table bodies.

```````````````````````````````` example
| h |
|---|
| a |
|---|
| b |
.
<table>
<thead>
<tr>
<th>h</th>
</tr>
</thead>
<tbody>
<tr>
<td>a</td>
</tr>
</tbody>
<tbody>
<tr>
<td>b</td>
</tr>
</tbody>
</table>
````````````````````````````````

Cells may contain inline markup. The beginning and the end of the cell act
as a beginning and end of a line.

```````````````````````````````` example
| *bold* | [[https://example.com][link]] |
|~code~|=verbatim=|
.
<table>
<tbody>
<tr>
<td><b>bold</b></td>
<td><a href="https://example.com">link</a></td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td><code>code</code></td>
<td><code>verbatim</code></td>
</tr>
</tbody>
</table>
````````````````````````````````

Alignment cookies. The row containing only the cookies is not exported.

```````````````````````````````` example
| <l> | <c> | <r10> |
| a   | b   | c     |
.
<table>
<tbody>
<tr>
<td align="left">a</td>
<td align="center">b</td>
<td align="right">c</td>
</tr>
</tbody>
</table>
````````````````````````````````

Tables may be indented, and they may appear in list items.

```````````````````````````````` example
- item
  | a |
  | b |
.
<ul>
<li><p>item</p>
<table>
<tbody>
<tr>
<td>a</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td>b</td>
</tr>
</tbody>
</table>
</li>
</ul>
````````````````````````````````

A table ends at the first line which does not start with `|`.

```````````````````````````````` example
| a |
foo
| b |
.
<table>
<tbody>
<tr>
<td>a</td>
</tr>
</tbody>
</table>
<p>foo</p>
<table>
<tbody>
<tr>
<td>b</td>
</tr>
</tbody>
</table>
````````````````````````````````
