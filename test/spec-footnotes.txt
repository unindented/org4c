# Footnotes

A footnote reference is written as `[fn:label]`. The footnote is defined
elsewhere by a line starting with `[fn:label]` at the column 0.

The footnotes are numbered in the order of their first reference, and all
the referenced ones are rendered at the end of the document.

```````````````````````````````` example
Foo[fn:1] and bar[fn:note].

[fn:note] The note.

[fn:1] The first.
.
<p>Foo<sup><a id="fnr.1" class="footref" href="#fn.1">1</a></sup> and bar<sup><a id="fnr.note" class="footref" href="#fn.note">2</a></sup>.</p>
<div id="footnotes">
<h2 class="footnotes">Footnotes:</h2>
<div id="text-footnotes">
<div class="footdef"><sup><a id="fn.1" class="footnum" href="#fnr.1">1</a></sup> <div class="footpara"><p>The first.</p>
</div></div>
<div class="footdef"><sup><a id="fn.note" class="footnum" href="#fnr.note">2</a></sup> <div class="footpara"><p>The note.</p>
</div></div>
</div>
</div>
````````````````````````````````

A definition may contain other elements. It ends before the next definition,
the next headline, or two consecutive blank lines.

```````````````````````````````` example
Foo[fn:1].

[fn:1] Para one.

- item


Not a part of the definition.
.
<p>Foo<sup><a id="fnr.1" class="footref" href="#fn.1">1</a></sup>.</p>
<p>Not a part of the definition.</p>
<div id="footnotes">
<h2 class="footnotes">Footnotes:</h2>
<div id="text-footnotes">
<div class="footdef"><sup><a id="fn.1" class="footnum" href="#fnr.1">1</a></sup> <div class="footpara"><p>Para one.</p>
<ul>
<li>item</li>
</ul>
</div></div>
</div>
</div>
````````````````````````````````

```````````````````````````````` example
Foo[fn:1].

[fn:1] Definition.
* Headline
Text.
.
<p>Foo<sup><a id="fnr.1" class="footref" href="#fn.1">1</a></sup>.</p>
<h1>Headline</h1>
<p>Text.</p>
<div id="footnotes">
<h2 class="footnotes">Footnotes:</h2>
<div id="text-footnotes">
<div class="footdef"><sup><a id="fn.1" class="footnum" href="#fnr.1">1</a></sup> <div class="footpara"><p>Definition.</p>
</div></div>
</div>
</div>
````````````````````````````````

Multiple references to the same footnote.

```````````````````````````````` example
A[fn:x] B[fn:x] C[fn:x]

[fn:x] X.
.
<p>A<sup><a id="fnr.x" class="footref" href="#fn.x">1</a></sup> B<sup><a id="fnr.x.1" class="footref" href="#fn.x">1</a></sup> C<sup><a id="fnr.x.2" class="footref" href="#fn.x">1</a></sup></p>
<div id="footnotes">
<h2 class="footnotes">Footnotes:</h2>
<div id="text-footnotes">
<div class="footdef"><sup><a id="fn.x" class="footnum" href="#fnr.x">1</a></sup> <div class="footpara"><p>X.</p>
</div></div>
</div>
</div>
````````````````````````````````

Inline definitions, named or anonymous.

```````````````````````````````` example
A[fn:inl:Inline *definition*.] B[fn::Anonymous one.] C[fn:inl]
.
<p>A<sup><a id="fnr.inl" class="footref" href="#fn.inl">1</a></sup> B<sup><a id="fnr.2" class="footref" href="#fn.2">2</a></sup> C<sup><a id="fnr.inl.1" class="footref" href="#fn.inl">1</a></sup></p>
<div id="footnotes">
<h2 class="footnotes">Footnotes:</h2>
<div id="text-footnotes">
<div class="footdef"><sup><a id="fn.inl" class="footnum" href="#fnr.inl">1</a></sup> <div class="footpara"><p>Inline <b>definition</b>.</p>
</div></div>
<div class="footdef"><sup><a id="fn.2" class="footnum" href="#fnr.2">2</a></sup> <div class="footpara"><p>Anonymous one.</p>
</div></div>
</div>
</div>
````````````````````````````````

The definition may contain balanced brackets.

```````````````````````````````` example
A[fn::See [[https://example.com][this]] [sic].]
.
<p>A<sup><a id="fnr.1" class="footref" href="#fn.1">1</a></sup></p>
<div id="footnotes">
<h2 class="footnotes">Footnotes:</h2>
<div id="text-footnotes">
<div class="footdef"><sup><a id="fn.1" class="footnum" href="#fnr.1">1</a></sup> <div class="footpara"><p>See <a href="https://example.com">this</a> [sic].</p>
</div></div>
</div>
</div>
````````````````````````````````

Unreferenced definitions are not rendered. References to undefined
footnotes are rendered (with an empty definition).

```````````````````````````````` example
[fn:unused] Unused.


Foo[fn:undefined].
.
<p>Foo<sup><a id="fnr.undefined" class="footref" href="#fn.undefined">1</a></sup>.</p>
<div id="footnotes">
<h2 class="footnotes">Footnotes:</h2>
<div id="text-footnotes">
<div class="footdef"><sup><a id="fn.undefined" class="footnum" href="#fnr.undefined">1</a></sup> <div class="footpara"></div></div>
</div>
</div>
````````````````````````````````

(Note a single blank line does not end the definition; so here the paragraph
is a part of the unreferenced definition.)

```````````````````````````````` example
[fn:unused] Unused.

Foo[fn:undefined].
.
````````````````````````````````

Footnotes referenced in footnote definitions.

```````````````````````````````` example
A[fn:1]

[fn:1] See also[fn:2].
[fn:2] Nested.
.
<p>A<sup><a id="fnr.1" class="footref" href="#fn.1">1</a></sup></p>
<div id="footnotes">
<h2 class="footnotes">Footnotes:</h2>
<div id="text-footnotes">
<div class="footdef"><sup><a id="fn.1" class="footnum" href="#fnr.1">1</a></sup> <div class="footpara"><p>See also<sup><a id="fnr.2" class="footref" href="#fn.2">2</a></sup>.</p>
</div></div>
<div class="footdef"><sup><a id="fn.2" class="footnum" href="#fnr.2">2</a></sup> <div class="footpara"><p>Nested.</p>
</div></div>
</div>
</div>
````````````````````````````````

The definition has to start at the column 0.

```````````````````````````````` example
 [fn:1] Not a definition.
.
<p><sup><a id="fnr.1" class="footref" href="#fn.1">1</a></sup> Not a definition.</p>
<div id="footnotes">
<h2 class="footnotes">Footnotes:</h2>
<div id="text-footnotes">
<div class="footdef"><sup><a id="fn.1" class="footnum" href="#fnr.1">1</a></sup> <div class="footpara"></div></div>
</div>
</div>
````````````````````````````````

With `--fno-footnotes` (`ORG_HTML_FLAG_NO_FOOTNOTES`), neither the references
nor the definitions are output (as Org's `f:nil`).

```````````````````````````````` example
A[fn:1] and B[fn::inline].

[fn:1] Defined.
.
<p>A and B.</p>
.
--fno-footnotes
````````````````````````````````
