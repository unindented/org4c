# ORG4C Specification

This file documents (and tests) how ORG4C parses the Org syntax and how
`org2html` renders it. The Org syntax itself is described in the document
"Org Syntax" (https://orgmode.org/worg/org-syntax.html); ORG4C tries to
follow it and to behave the same way as Emacs `org-element` parser does.

The HTML output resembles the output of Emacs `ox-html` exporter, but it is
intentionally simpler: No generated `id` attributes (e.g. of headlines), no
`<div class="outline-N">` wrappers and so on.

Each example consists of the Org input, a line with a single dot, and the
expected HTML output; optionally followed by another line with a single dot
and the command line options of `org2html` to use. (Tabs are shown as `→`.)

# Headlines

A headline is a line starting with one or more stars, followed by a space.
The count of stars is its level.

```````````````````````````````` example
* Level 1
** Level 2
*** Level 3
.
<h1>Level 1</h1>
<h2>Level 2</h2>
<h3>Level 3</h3>
````````````````````````````````

Levels deeper than 6 are rendered as `<h6>`.

```````````````````````````````` example
******* Level 7
.
<h6>Level 7</h6>
````````````````````````````````

With `ORG_HTML_FLAG_TOPLEVEL_H2`, a headline of level N is rendered as
`<hN+1>` (still capped at `<h6>`), as ox-html does, so that `<h1>` is left
for the document title.

```````````````````````````````` example
* Level 1
** Level 2
*** Level 3
**** Level 4
***** Level 5
****** Level 6
.
<h2>Level 1</h2>
<h3>Level 2</h3>
<h4>Level 3</h4>
<h5>Level 4</h5>
<h6>Level 5</h6>
<h6>Level 6</h6>
.
--ftoplevel-h2
````````````````````````````````

The title of an inline task is not affected.

```````````````````````````````` example
* Head
*************** Task
.
<h2>Head</h2>
<div class="inlinetask">
<b>Task</b><br>
</div>
.
--ftoplevel-h2 --finlinetasks
````````````````````````````````

The stars must be at the very beginning of the line and they must be
followed by a space:

```````````````````````````````` example
*Not a headline
 **Not a headline either
.
<p>*Not a headline
**Not a headline either</p>
````````````````````````````````

A headline may start with a TODO keyword (`TODO` or `DONE`), then a priority
cookie, and it may end with tags.

```````````````````````````````` example
* TODO [#A] Write the spec :work:urgent:
* DONE Done
.
<h1><span class="todo TODO">TODO</span> <span class="priority">[A]</span> Write the spec&#xa0;&#xa0;&#xa0;<span class="tag"><span class="work">work</span>&#xa0;<span class="urgent">urgent</span></span></h1>
<h1><span class="done DONE">DONE</span> Done</h1>
````````````````````````````````

```````````````````````````````` example
* TODOs are not a keyword
* [#B] Just priority
* Title :not:tags: here
.
<h1>TODOs are not a keyword</h1>
<h1><span class="priority">[B]</span> Just priority</h1>
<h1>Title :not:tags: here</h1>
````````````````````````````````

The TODO keywords can be customized by `#+TODO:` (or `#+SEQ_TODO:`,
`#+TYP_TODO:`). The keywords after `|` are the "done" ones; if there is no
`|`, only the last keyword is. Then `TODO` and `DONE` are not keywords
anymore (unless listed).

```````````````````````````````` example
#+TODO: NEXT(n) | FINISHED(f!) CANCELED
#+TYP_TODO: WAIT
* NEXT a
* FINISHED b
* CANCELED c
* WAIT d
* TODO e
.
<h1><span class="todo NEXT">NEXT</span> a</h1>
<h1><span class="done FINISHED">FINISHED</span> b</h1>
<h1><span class="done CANCELED">CANCELED</span> c</h1>
<h1><span class="done WAIT">WAIT</span> d</h1>
<h1>TODO e</h1>
````````````````````````````````

The title may contain any inline markup.

```````````````````````````````` example
* Some /emphasis/ and a [[https://example.com][link]]
.
<h1>Some <i>emphasis</i> and a <a href="https://example.com">link</a></h1>
````````````````````````````````

Empty headline.

```````````````````````````````` example
* 
.
<h1></h1>
````````````````````````````````

Headlines starting with the keyword `COMMENT`, as well as headlines tagged
`noexport`, are not exported, together with all their contents (including
their subsections).

```````````````````````````````` example
* Visible
* COMMENT Hidden
secret
** Hidden subsection
* Hidden too :noexport:
secret
* Visible again
.
<h1>Visible</h1>
<h1>Visible again</h1>
````````````````````````````````

With `ORG_FLAG_SKIPCOMMENTED` and `ORG_FLAG_SKIPNOEXPORT` (which `org_html()`
always uses), such subtrees are not reported at all. That matters e.g. for
the footnotes referenced only from them.

```````````````````````````````` example
A[fn:1]
* COMMENT Hidden
B[fn:2]

[fn:1] One.
[fn:2] Two.
.
<p>A<sup><a id="fnr.1" class="footref" href="#fn.1">1</a></sup></p>
<div id="footnotes">
<h2 class="footnotes">Footnotes:</h2>
<div id="text-footnotes">
<div class="footdef"><sup><a id="fn.1" class="footnum" href="#fnr.1">1</a></sup> <div class="footpara"><p>One.</p>
</div></div>
</div>
</div>
````````````````````````````````

With `ORG_FLAG_INLINETASKS`, headlines of level 15 or deeper are inline
tasks, optionally terminated with a line `*************** END`.

```````````````````````````````` example
* Head
*************** TODO Task :tag:
Body
*************** END
Text
*************** One-line task
.
<h1>Head</h1>
<div class="inlinetask">
<b><span class="todo TODO">TODO</span> Task&#xa0;&#xa0;&#xa0;<span class="tag"><span class="tag">tag</span></span></b><br>
<p>Body</p>
</div>
<p>Text</p>
<div class="inlinetask">
<b>One-line task</b><br>
</div>
.
--finlinetasks
````````````````````````````````

# Planning and Property Drawers

A planning line (`SCHEDULED:`, `DEADLINE:`, `CLOSED:`) directly following a
headline, and a property drawer directly following the headline (or its
planning line), are not exported.

```````````````````````````````` example
* Task
SCHEDULED: <2024-01-01 Mon>
:PROPERTIES:
:ID: 1234
:END:
Body.
.
<h1>Task</h1>
<p>Body.</p>
````````````````````````````````

Elsewhere, it is just a normal text (or a normal drawer).

```````````````````````````````` example
SCHEDULED: <2024-01-01 Mon>
:PROPERTIES:
:ID: 1234
:END:
.
<p>SCHEDULED: <span class="timestamp-wrapper"><span class="timestamp">&lt;2024-01-01 Mon&gt;</span></span></p>
<p>:ID: 1234</p>
````````````````````````````````

# Paragraphs

Consecutive lines of text form a paragraph. Paragraphs are separated by blank
lines or by any other element.

```````````````````````````````` example
aaa
bbb

ccc
.
<p>aaa
bbb</p>
<p>ccc</p>
````````````````````````````````

```````````````````````````````` example
  aaa
    bbb
.
<p>aaa
bbb</p>
````````````````````````````````

```````````````````````````````` example
aaa
* Headline
bbb
.
<p>aaa</p>
<h1>Headline</h1>
<p>bbb</p>
````````````````````````````````

Hard line break is written as `\\` at the end of a line.

```````````````````````````````` example
foo\\
bar
.
<p>foo<br>
bar</p>
````````````````````````````````

```````````````````````````````` example
foo \\
bar\\
.
<p>foo <br>
bar</p>
````````````````````````````````

```````````````````````````````` example
foo\\\
bar
.
<p>foo\\\
bar</p>
````````````````````````````````

Special strings: As `ox-html` does, `org2html` renders `--` as an en dash,
`---` as an em dash, `...` as an ellipsis and `\-` as a soft hyphen. (This
can be disabled.)

```````````````````````````````` example
a -- b --- c ... d\-e
.
<p>a &ndash; b &mdash; c &hellip; d&shy;e</p>
````````````````````````````````

The dashes have to be followed by a character other than `-`, or by the end
of line; so e.g. not by an object, nor by the end of a table cell or of a
headline.

```````````````````````````````` example
a--
b---
c----
d--*e* f--\alpha

| g-- |
.
<p>a&ndash;
b&mdash;
c-&mdash;
d--<b>e</b> f--&alpha;</p>
<table>
<tbody>
<tr>
<td>g--</td>
</tr>
</tbody>
</table>
````````````````````````````````

```````````````````````````````` example
a --\\
b
.
<p>a --<br>
b</p>
````````````````````````````````

```````````````````````````````` example
a -- b --- c ... d\-e
.
<p>a -- b --- c ... d\-e</p>
.
--fverbatim-special-strings
````````````````````````````````

# Emphasis and Monospace

Org recognizes these kinds of emphasis:

```````````````````````````````` example
*bold* /italic/ _underline_ +strike-through+ =verbatim= ~code~
.
<p><b>bold</b> <i>italic</i> <span class="underline">underline</span> <del>strike-through</del> <code>verbatim</code> <code>code</code></p>
````````````````````````````````

The opening marker has to be preceded by a whitespace, one of `-({'"`, or
the beginning of the line; and it has to be followed by a non-whitespace.
The closing marker has to be preceded by a non-whitespace, and followed by a
whitespace, one of `-.,;:!?')}["\`, or the end of line.

```````````````````````````````` example
2*3*4 a/b/c snake_case_name 1+2+3 a=b=c
.
<p>2*3*4 a/b/c snake_case_name 1+2+3 a=b=c</p>
````````````````````````````````

```````````````````````````````` example
(*bold*) -/italic/- '_u_' "=v="
.
<p>(<b>bold</b>) -<i>italic</i>- &#x27;<span class="underline">u</span>&#x27; &quot;<code>v</code>&quot;</p>
````````````````````````````````

```````````````````````````````` example
*bold*, /italic/. _u_; +s+: =v=! ~c~?
.
<p><b>bold</b>, <i>italic</i>. <span class="underline">u</span>; <del>s</del>: <code>v</code>! <code>c</code>?</p>
````````````````````````````````

```````````````````````````````` example
a * not bold * b
.
<p>a * not bold * b</p>
````````````````````````````````

Org does not use the CommonMark's delimiter-run algorithm. A span is formed
by an opening marker and the nearest possible closing marker of the same
kind, regardless of any other markers in between.

```````````````````````````````` example
*a *b* c*
.
<p><b>a *b</b> c*</p>
````````````````````````````````

```````````````````````````````` example
/a *b/ c*
.
<p><i>a *b</i> c*</p>
````````````````````````````````

```````````````````````````````` example
*a*b* and *a *b*
.
<p><b>a*b</b> and <b>a *b</b></p>
````````````````````````````````

The contents of verbatim and code is not parsed.

```````````````````````````````` example
=a *b= c* ~*x* /y/~
.
<p><code>a *b</code> c* <code>*x* /y/</code></p>
````````````````````````````````

The contents of other spans is parsed as if it were a stand-alone text:
Its beginning and end act as a beginning and end of a line.

```````````````````````````````` example
**x** and */x/*
.
<p><b><b>x</b></b> and <b><i>x</i></b></p>
````````````````````````````````

```````````````````````````````` example
*bold /italic _underline_/*
.
<p><b>bold <i>italic <span class="underline">underline</span></i></b></p>
````````````````````````````````

Emphasis may span over multiple lines of a paragraph.

```````````````````````````````` example
*multi
line* bold
.
<p><b>multi
line</b> bold</p>
````````````````````````````````

The contents must not be empty.

```````````````````````````````` example
a ** b == c ~~ d
.
<p>a ** b == c ~~ d</p>
````````````````````````````````

# Entities

Org entities (see the variable `org-entities` in Emacs) are written as
`\name`, optionally followed by `{}` (which allows gluing them to the
following text).

```````````````````````````````` example
\alpha \alpha{}beta \to \copy{} \nbsp{} \there4 \frac12
.
<p>&alpha; &alpha;beta &rarr; &copy; &nbsp; &there4; &frac12;</p>
````````````````````````````````

`\_` followed by spaces is a (non-breaking) white space.

```````````````````````````````` example
a\_  b
.
<p>a&ensp;&ensp;b</p>
````````````````````````````````

Unknown names are not entities (they are LaTeX fragments, see below).

```````````````````````````````` example
\notanentity
.
<p>\notanentity</p>
````````````````````````````````

LaTeX fragments are passed to the output (so they may be processed by
e.g. MathJax). As `ox-html` does, `$...$` is converted to `\(...\)` and
`$$...$$` to `\[...\]`.

```````````````````````````````` example
\(a < b\) \[x^2\] $y$ $$z$$ \frac{1}{2} \cmd*[opt]{arg}
.
<p>\(a &lt; b\) \[x^2\] \(y\) \[z\] \frac{1}{2} \cmd*[opt]{arg}</p>
````````````````````````````````

The rules for `$` are strict: The contents must not begin with a whitespace
(nor `,.;`), must not end with a whitespace (nor `,.`), and the closing `$`
must not be followed by an alphanumeric character.

```````````````````````````````` example
$5 and $10, $ a$, $a $, $x$y, $x$.
.
<p>$5 and $10, $ a$, $a $, $x$y, \(x\).</p>
````````````````````````````````

```````````````````````````````` example
LaTeX can be disabled: $x$ \(y\) \alpha
.
<p>LaTeX can be disabled: $x$ \(y\) &alpha;</p>
.
--fno-latex
````````````````````````````````

LaTeX environments.

```````````````````````````````` example
\begin{equation}
  x = 1
\end{equation}
.
\begin{equation}
  x = 1
\end{equation}
````````````````````````````````

```````````````````````````````` example
\begin{equation}
no end
.
<p>\begin{equation}
no end</p>
````````````````````````````````

# Timestamps

Active (`<...>`) and inactive (`[...]`) timestamps, including ranges.

```````````````````````````````` example
<2024-01-31 Wed> [2024-01-31 Wed 10:00] <2024-01-01 Mon>--<2024-01-03 Wed> <2024-01-01 Mon 10:00-11:00 +1w>
.
<p><span class="timestamp-wrapper"><span class="timestamp">&lt;2024-01-31 Wed&gt;</span></span> <span class="timestamp-wrapper"><span class="timestamp">[2024-01-31 Wed 10:00]</span></span> <span class="timestamp-wrapper"><span class="timestamp">&lt;2024-01-01 Mon&gt;&ndash;&lt;2024-01-03 Wed&gt;</span></span> <span class="timestamp-wrapper"><span class="timestamp">&lt;2024-01-01 Mon 10:00-11:00 +1w&gt;</span></span></p>
````````````````````````````````

```````````````````````````````` example
<2024-1-31> [2024-01-31 no end
.
<p>&lt;2024-1-31&gt; [2024-01-31 no end</p>
````````````````````````````````

# Other Objects

Statistics cookies.

```````````````````````````````` example
* Tasks [1/3] [33%]
.
<h1>Tasks <code>[1/3]</code> <code>[33%]</code></h1>
````````````````````````````````

Targets. They are the targets of the fuzzy links.

```````````````````````````````` example
<<here>> and [[here][a link to it]]
.
<p><a id="here"></a> and <a href="#here">a link to it</a></p>
````````````````````````````````

Export snippets. Only the ones for HTML are rendered.

```````````````````````````````` example
@@html:<kbd>@@Ctrl@@html:</kbd>@@ @@latex:\LaTeX@@
.
<p><kbd>Ctrl</kbd> </p>
````````````````````````````````

Inline source blocks.

```````````````````````````````` example
src_python{print(1)} and src_sh[:exports code]{ls -l}
.
<p><code class="src src-python">print(1)</code> and <code class="src src-sh">ls -l</code></p>
````````````````````````````````

# Macros

Macros are defined by `#+MACRO: name template` (the first definition wins),
where `$1`, `$2`, ... are replaced by the arguments. The expansion is parsed
as an Org text.

```````````````````````````````` example
#+MACRO: greet Hello, *$1* and $2!
{{{greet(World, Bob)}}} {{{greet(a\, b)}}} {{{greet}}}
.
<p>Hello, <b>World</b> and  Bob! Hello, <b>a, b</b> and ! Hello, ** and !</p>
````````````````````````````````

Whitespace in the arguments is collapsed; an odd count of backslashes
escapes the comma.

```````````````````````````````` example
#+MACRO: m [$1|$2]
{{{m(  a  b ,
 c)}}} {{{m(a\\,b)}}}
.
<p>[a b | c] [a\|b]</p>
````````````````````````````````

Macros may use other macros.

```````````````````````````````` example
#+MACRO: inner <$1>
#+MACRO: outer ({{{inner($1)}}})
{{{outer(x)}}}
.
<p>(&lt;x&gt;)</p>
````````````````````````````````

Built-in macros: `title`, `author`, `email`, `date`, `keyword(NAME)` and the
counter `n` (`n(name)`, `n(name,-)` to just show it, `n(name,5)` to set it,
`n(name,reset)` to reset it). The macros `time`, `property`, `input-file` and
`modification-time` are not supported and expand to nothing.

```````````````````````````````` example
#+TITLE: The /Title/
#+AUTHOR: Me
{{{title}}} by {{{author}}}, {{{keyword(AUTHOR)}}}. {{{n}}} {{{n}}} {{{n(x,5)}}} {{{n(x)}}} {{{n(x,-)}}} {{{n(x,reset)}}} {{{time(%Y)}}}
.
<p>The <i>Title</i> by Me, Me. 1 2 5 6 6 1 </p>
````````````````````````````````

Unknown macros, as well as the macros defined by an `(eval ...)` template
(ORG4C cannot evaluate Emacs Lisp), are kept as they are.

```````````````````````````````` example
#+MACRO: ev (eval (upcase $1))
{{{unknown(x)}}} {{{ev(y)}}}
.
<p>{{{unknown(x)}}} {{{ev(y)}}}</p>
````````````````````````````````

# Radio Targets

Radio target `<<<text>>>` makes all the occurrences of its text (as a whole
word, case-insensitively) links to it.

```````````````````````````````` example
A <<<radio target>>> here.

The radio target, the Radio
Target, not radio targets.
.
<p>A <a id="radio target">radio target</a> here.</p>
<p>The <a href="#radio%20target">radio target</a>, the <a href="#radio%20target">Radio
Target</a>, not radio targets.</p>
````````````````````````````````

# Citations

Citations are rendered as their keys (ORG4C has no citation processor).

```````````````````````````````` example
[cite:@smith2020] [cite/t:see @doe p. 5; @roe] [cite:global; @a; @b; end]
.
<p><span class="citation">(<cite>smith2020</cite>)</span> <span class="citation">(see <cite>doe</cite> p. 5; <cite>roe</cite>)</span> <span class="citation">(global <cite>a</cite>; <cite>b</cite> end)</span></p>
````````````````````````````````

```````````````````````````````` example
[cite:no key] [cite:@a
.
<p>[cite:no key] [cite:@a</p>
````````````````````````````````

# Inline Babel Calls

ORG4C never evaluates any code, so inline Babel calls produce nothing.

```````````````````````````````` example
Result: call_square(4) and call_f[:x 1](2)[:results raw].
.
<p>Result:  and .</p>
````````````````````````````````

# Links

A bracket link has the form `[[TARGET][DESCRIPTION]]` or `[[TARGET]]`.

```````````````````````````````` example
[[https://example.com][Example]] and [[https://example.com]]
.
<p><a href="https://example.com">Example</a> and <a href="https://example.com">https://example.com</a></p>
````````````````````````````````

The description may contain inline markup.

```````````````````````````````` example
[[https://example.com][*bold* /italic/ and ~code~]]
.
<p><a href="https://example.com"><b>bold</b> <i>italic</i> and <code>code</code></a></p>
````````````````````````````````

Links to files. Links to Org files are converted to links to the exported
HTML files.

```````````````````````````````` example
[[file:notes.txt][notes]] [[file:other.org][other]] [[file:other.org::*Heading][heading]] [[./rel.org]]
.
<p><a href="notes.txt">notes</a> <a href="other.html">other</a> <a href="other.html">heading</a> <a href="./rel.html">./rel.org</a></p>
````````````````````````````````

Links without a description pointing to an image are rendered as the
image. A paragraph consisting only of such an image is a figure.

```````````````````````````````` example
[[file:image.png]]

Text [[file:image.png]].
.
<div class="figure">
<p><img src="image.png" alt="image.png">
</p>
</div>
<p>Text <img src="image.png" alt="image.png">.</p>
````````````````````````````````

```````````````````````````````` example
[[file:image.png]] [[https://example.com/pic.JPG]] [[file:image.png][not an image]]
.
<p><img src="image.png" alt="image.png"> <img src="https://example.com/pic.JPG" alt="pic.JPG"> <a href="image.png">not an image</a></p>
````````````````````````````````

Internal links.

```````````````````````````````` example
[[#custom-id][by custom id]] [[id:1234][by id]] [[*Some Heading]] [[target]]
.
<p><a href="#custom-id">by custom id</a> <a href="#ID-1234">by id</a> <a href="#Some%20Heading">*Some Heading</a> <a href="#target">target</a></p>
````````````````````````````````

Other link types.

```````````````````````````````` example
[[mailto:me@example.com][mail]] [[doi:10.1000/182][doi]] [[foo:bar]]
.
<p><a href="mailto:me@example.com">mail</a> <a href="https://doi.org/10.1000/182">doi</a> <a href="#foo:bar">foo:bar</a></p>
````````````````````````````````

Angle links.

```````````````````````````````` example
<https://example.com/a b> <mailto:me@example.com> <notalink>
.
<p><a href="https://example.com/a%20b">https://example.com/a b</a> <a href="mailto:me@example.com">mailto:me@example.com</a> &lt;notalink&gt;</p>
````````````````````````````````

Plain links are recognized without the brackets (for some well-known link
types). Trailing punctuation is not a part of the link.

```````````````````````````````` example
See https://example.com/path?a=1. Or mailto:me@example.com, or ftp://x.org/!
.
<p>See <a href="https://example.com/path?a=1">https://example.com/path?a=1</a>. Or <a href="mailto:me@example.com">mailto:me@example.com</a>, or <a href="ftp://x.org/">ftp://x.org/</a>!</p>
````````````````````````````````

```````````````````````````````` example
xhttps://example.com (no link) and [[https://a.com][https://b.com]]
.
<p>xhttps://example.com (no link) and <a href="https://a.com">https://b.com</a></p>
````````````````````````````````

```````````````````````````````` example
Plain links can be disabled: https://example.com
.
<p>Plain links can be disabled: https://example.com</p>
.
--fno-plain-links
````````````````````````````````

Unterminated or malformed links are just text.

```````````````````````````````` example
[[foo] and [[]] and [[a[b]]
.
<p>[[foo] and [[]] and [[a[b]]</p>
````````````````````````````````

The description is anything up to the first `]]`.

```````````````````````````````` example
[[a][b] c]]
.
<p><a href="#a">b] c</a></p>
````````````````````````````````

# Plain Lists

A list item starts with a bullet (`-`, `+` or `*`) or an ordered bullet
(e.g. `1.` or `1)`), followed by a whitespace. (The bullet `*` is recognized
only if indented, otherwise it would be a headline.)

```````````````````````````````` example
- foo
- bar
.
<ul>
<li>foo</li>
<li>bar</li>
</ul>
````````````````````````````````

```````````````````````````````` example
+ foo
+ bar
.
<ul>
<li>foo</li>
<li>bar</li>
</ul>
````````````````````````````````

```````````````````````````````` example
  * foo
  * bar
.
<ul>
<li>foo</li>
<li>bar</li>
</ul>
````````````````````````````````

```````````````````````````````` example
1. foo
2. bar
.
<ol>
<li>foo</li>
<li>bar</li>
</ol>
````````````````````````````````

```````````````````````````````` example
1) foo
2) bar
.
<ol>
<li>foo</li>
<li>bar</li>
</ol>
````````````````````````````````

The type of the list is determined by its first item.

```````````````````````````````` example
- foo
1. bar
.
<ul>
<li>foo</li>
<li>bar</li>
</ul>
````````````````````````````````

The contents of the item has to be indented more than its bullet.

```````````````````````````````` example
- foo
  bar
baz
.
<ul>
<li>foo
bar</li>
</ul>
<p>baz</p>
````````````````````````````````

```````````````````````````````` example
- foo
  - bar
    baz
  qux
- quux
.
<ul>
<li><p>foo</p>
<ul>
<li>bar
baz</li>
</ul>
<p>qux</p>
</li>
<li>quux</li>
</ul>
````````````````````````````````

A paragraph is not wrapped in `<p>` if it is the only contents of the item
(ignoring its sub-list).

```````````````````````````````` example
- foo

  bar
- baz
  - sub
.
<ul>
<li><p>foo</p>
<p>bar</p>
</li>
<li>baz<ul>
<li>sub</li>
</ul>
</li>
</ul>
````````````````````````````````

A single blank line does not end the list, but two consecutive blank lines
do.

```````````````````````````````` example
- foo

- bar


- baz
.
<ul>
<li>foo</li>
<li>bar</li>
</ul>
<ul>
<li>baz</li>
</ul>
````````````````````````````````

Checkboxes and counters.

```````````````````````````````` example
- [ ] todo
- [X] done
- [-] partial
.
<ul>
<li class="off"><code>[&#xa0;]</code> todo</li>
<li class="on"><code>[X]</code> done</li>
<li class="trans"><code>[-]</code> partial</li>
</ul>
````````````````````````````````

```````````````````````````````` example
1. foo
2. [@5] bar
3. [@7] [X] baz
.
<ol>
<li>foo</li>
<li value="5">bar</li>
<li class="on" value="7"><code>[X]</code> baz</li>
</ol>
````````````````````````````````

Description lists.

```````````````````````````````` example
- term :: definition
- [X] term 2 :: definition 2
  more
- :: no term
.
<dl>
<dt>term</dt>
<dd>definition</dd>
<dt class="on"><code>[X]</code> term 2</dt>
<dd>definition 2
more</dd>
<dt></dt>
<dd>:: no term</dd>
</dl>
````````````````````````````````

The last `::` separates the term.

```````````````````````````````` example
- a :: b :: c
.
<dl>
<dt>a :: b</dt>
<dd>c</dd>
</dl>
````````````````````````````````

Empty items.

```````````````````````````````` example
-
- [ ]
.
<ul>
<li></li>
<li class="off"><code>[&#xa0;]</code> </li>
</ul>
````````````````````````````````

Lists interrupt paragraphs and vice versa.

```````````````````````````````` example
foo
- bar
baz
.
<p>foo</p>
<ul>
<li>bar</li>
</ul>
<p>baz</p>
````````````````````````````````

Elements inside the list items. Note the lines of a block are not affected
by the indentation.

```````````````````````````````` example
- item
  #+begin_quote
quoted
  #+end_quote
- next
.
<ul>
<li><p>item</p>
<blockquote>
<p>quoted</p>
</blockquote>
</li>
<li>next</li>
</ul>
````````````````````````````````

```````````````````````````````` example
- item
  #+begin_src sh
echo hello
  #+end_src
.
<ul>
<li><p>item</p>
<pre><code class="language-sh">echo hello
</code></pre>
</li>
</ul>
````````````````````````````````

# Blocks

Source blocks. The common indentation is removed.

```````````````````````````````` example
#+begin_src python :results output
  def f():
      return 1
#+end_src
.
<pre><code class="language-python">def f():
    return 1
</code></pre>
````````````````````````````````

```````````````````````````````` example
#+BEGIN_SRC
no language
#+END_SRC
.
<pre><code>no language
</code></pre>
````````````````````````````````

The header argument `:exports` is respected. As ORG4C never evaluates the
code, `:exports results` exports nothing (the same as `:exports none`).

```````````````````````````````` example
#+begin_src sh :exports none
secret
#+end_src
#+begin_src sh :exports results
ls
#+end_src
#+begin_src sh :exports code
ls
#+end_src
.
<pre><code class="language-sh">ls
</code></pre>
````````````````````````````````

Line numbers: `-n` (optionally with the first number), `+n` to continue
the numbering of the previous numbered block.

```````````````````````````````` example
#+begin_src sh -n
echo a
echo b
#+end_src
#+begin_src sh +n
echo c
#+end_src
#+begin_example -n 9
x
y
#+end_example
.
<pre><code class="language-sh"><span class="linenr">1: </span>echo a
<span class="linenr">2: </span>echo b
</code></pre>
<pre><code class="language-sh"><span class="linenr">3: </span>echo c
</code></pre>
<pre class="example"><span class="linenr"> 9: </span>x
<span class="linenr">10: </span>y
</pre>
````````````````````````````````

Code references: A label `(ref:name)` at the end of a line (the format may
be changed by `-l`). The switch `-r` removes the labels; the links to them
then show the line numbers.

```````````````````````````````` example
#+begin_src sh -n
echo a (ref:first)
echo b
#+end_src
#+begin_src sh -n 10 -r -l "[%s]"
x [second]
#+end_src
See [[(first)]], [[(second)]] and [[(first)][this]].
.
<pre><code class="language-sh"><span id="coderef-first" class="coderef-off"><span class="linenr">1: </span>echo a (first)</span>
<span class="linenr">2: </span>echo b
</code></pre>
<pre><code class="language-sh"><span id="coderef-second" class="coderef-off"><span class="linenr">10: </span>x</span>
</code></pre>
<p>See <a href="#coderef-first" class="coderef">first</a>, <a href="#coderef-second" class="coderef">10</a> and <a href="#coderef-first" class="coderef">this</a>.</p>
````````````````````````````````

Lines starting with `*` or `#+` are escaped with a comma in the source
blocks. The comma is removed.

```````````````````````````````` example
#+begin_src org
,* Headline
,#+begin_src
,,* Still escaped
, no escape
#+end_src
.
<pre><code class="language-org">* Headline
#+begin_src
,* Still escaped
, no escape
</code></pre>
````````````````````````````````

The block names are case-insensitive, but `#+END_name` has to match the
name of the block.

```````````````````````````````` example
#+Begin_Example
<b>x</b>
#+eNd_eXaMpLe
.
<pre class="example">&lt;b&gt;x&lt;/b&gt;
</pre>
````````````````````````````````

If there is no matching `#+END_name`, it is not a block.

```````````````````````````````` example
#+begin_src
code
#+end_example
.
<p>#+begin_src
code
#+end_example</p>
````````````````````````````````

Blocks cannot span over a headline.

```````````````````````````````` example
#+begin_src
code
* Headline
#+end_src
.
<p>#+begin_src
code</p>
<h1>Headline</h1>
<p>#+end_src</p>
````````````````````````````````

Example blocks.

```````````````````````````````` example
#+begin_example
  foo
    bar
#+end_example
.
<pre class="example">foo
  bar
</pre>
````````````````````````````````

Quote and center blocks contain other elements.

```````````````````````````````` example
#+begin_quote
foo /bar/

- baz
#+end_quote
.
<blockquote>
<p>foo <i>bar</i></p>
<ul>
<li>baz</li>
</ul>
</blockquote>
````````````````````````````````

```````````````````````````````` example
#+begin_center
centered
#+end_center
.
<div class="org-center">
<p>centered</p>
</div>
````````````````````````````````

Nested blocks.

```````````````````````````````` example
#+begin_quote
#+begin_center
foo
#+end_center
#+end_quote
.
<blockquote>
<div class="org-center">
<p>foo</p>
</div>
</blockquote>
````````````````````````````````

Verse blocks keep the line breaks and the relative indentation.

```````````````````````````````` example
#+begin_verse
  Roses are red,
    violets are blue.

  The end.
#+end_verse
.
<p class="verse">Roses are red,<br>
&#xa0;&#xa0;violets are blue.<br>
<br>
The end.</p>
````````````````````````````````

Special blocks (any other name) are rendered as `<div>` with the class of
the block name.

```````````````````````````````` example
#+begin_note
Some *note*.
#+end_note
.
<div class="note">
<p>Some <b>note</b>.</p>
</div>
````````````````````````````````

Export blocks are passed to the output verbatim, if they are meant for
HTML. Others are ignored.

```````````````````````````````` example
#+begin_export html
<b>raw</b>
#+end_export
#+begin_export latex
\LaTeX
#+end_export
.
<b>raw</b>
````````````````````````````````

```````````````````````````````` example
#+begin_export html
<b>raw</b>
#+end_export
.
.
--fno-export-blocks
````````````````````````````````

Dynamic blocks. The contents is rendered as it is.

```````````````````````````````` example
#+BEGIN: clocktable :scope file
| a |
Text
#+END:

#+BEGIN: unterminated
.
<table>
<tbody>
<tr>
<td>a</td>
</tr>
</tbody>
</table>
<p>Text</p>
<p>#+BEGIN: unterminated</p>
````````````````````````````````

Comment blocks are not exported.

```````````````````````````````` example
#+begin_comment
secret
#+end_comment
visible
.
<p>visible</p>
````````````````````````````````

# Drawers

Drawers are exported without any wrapper. (LOGBOOK drawers are not exported
at all.)

```````````````````````````````` example
:MYDRAWER:
foo
:END:
:LOGBOOK:
- State "DONE"
:END:
.
<p>foo</p>
````````````````````````````````

Without `:END:`, it is not a drawer.

```````````````````````````````` example
:FOO:
bar
.
<p>:FOO:
bar</p>
````````````````````````````````

# Keywords and Comments

Keywords are not exported.

```````````````````````````````` example
#+TITLE: The Title
#+OPTIONS: toc:nil
Text
.
<p>Text</p>
````````````````````````````````

Comment lines start with `#` followed by a space (or the end of line).

```````````````````````````````` example
# comment
  # indented comment
#
text
#not a comment
.
<p>text
#not a comment</p>
````````````````````````````````

Affiliated keywords (e.g. `#+CAPTION:`, `#+NAME:`, `#+ATTR_HTML:`) belong to
the element directly following them. The caption is reported as the first
child of the element; the renderer uses the captions of tables, source
blocks and figures. The renderer also uses `#+NAME:` and `#+ATTR_HTML:` (see
below).

```````````````````````````````` example
#+CAPTION: The /picture/
[[file:image.png]]

#+CAPTION: Ignored for a normal paragraph
Text
.
<div class="figure">
<p><img src="image.png" alt="image.png">
</p>
<p><span class="figure-number">Figure 1: </span>The <i>picture</i></p>
</div>
<p>Text</p>
````````````````````````````````

```````````````````````````````` example
#+CAPTION: The table
#+NAME: tbl
| a |

#+CAPTION: The code
#+begin_src sh
ls
#+end_src
.
<table id="tbl">
<caption class="t-above"><span class="table-number">Table 1:</span> The table</caption>
<tbody>
<tr>
<td>a</td>
</tr>
</tbody>
</table>
<label class="org-src-name"><span class="listing-number">Listing 1: </span>The code</label><pre><code class="language-sh">ls
</code></pre>
````````````````````````````````

If there is a blank line after the keyword, it is not affiliated.

```````````````````````````````` example
#+CAPTION: Orphan

| a |
.
<table>
<tbody>
<tr>
<td>a</td>
</tr>
</tbody>
</table>
````````````````````````````````

The name (`#+NAME:`) of an element is used as its `id`, so that the links to
it work. Unlike `ox-html`, which does so only for some elements, ORG4C does so
also for paragraphs, lists, verse and center blocks and fixed-width areas.
(Drawers, dynamic blocks, horizontal rules and LaTeX environments get no
`id`.)

```````````````````````````````` example
#+NAME: tbl
| a |

#+NAME: code
#+begin_src sh
ls
#+end_src

#+NAME: par
A paragraph.

See [[tbl]], [[code]] and [[par]].
.
<table id="tbl">
<tbody>
<tr>
<td>a</td>
</tr>
</tbody>
</table>
<pre id="code"><code class="language-sh">ls
</code></pre>
<p id="par">A paragraph.</p>
<p>See <a href="#tbl">tbl</a>, <a href="#code">code</a> and <a href="#par">par</a>.</p>
````````````````````````````````

The attributes given by `#+ATTR_HTML:` are added to tables, paragraphs, lists,
quotes, example blocks and special blocks (the elements `ox-html` adds them
to). A class is appended to the class the element has anyway.

```````````````````````````````` example
#+ATTR_HTML: :class wide :border 1
| a |

#+ATTR_HTML: :class tip
#+begin_quote
q
#+end_quote

#+ATTR_HTML: :class steps
1. a

#+ATTR_HTML: :class out
#+begin_example
e
#+end_example

#+ATTR_HTML: :class warning
#+begin_note
n
#+end_note
.
<table class="wide" border="1">
<tbody>
<tr>
<td>a</td>
</tr>
</tbody>
</table>
<blockquote class="tip">
<p>q</p>
</blockquote>
<ol class="steps">
<li>a</li>
</ol>
<pre class="example out">e
</pre>
<div class="note warning">
<p>n</p>
</div>
````````````````````````````````

As in `ox-html`, the attributes of a paragraph also go to its first link. This
is how the attributes of an image are given (`:alt` replaces the default
alternative text). The attributes of a figure go only to its image; its
name goes to the figure.

```````````````````````````````` example
#+NAME: fig
#+CAPTION: Cat
#+ATTR_HTML: :width 300 :alt A cat
[[file:cat.png]]

#+ATTR_HTML: :class c
See [[https://a.org][A]], [[file:b.png]] and [[https://c.org][C]].
.
<div class="figure" id="fig">
<p><img src="cat.png" alt="A cat" width="300">
</p>
<p><span class="figure-number">Figure 1: </span>Cat</p>
</div>
<p class="c">See <a href="https://a.org" class="c">A</a>, <img src="b.png" alt="b.png"> and <a href="https://c.org">C</a>.</p>
````````````````````````````````

Any text before the first key is ignored, and so are the attributes without
any value or with the value `nil`. The values are output as they are (only
escaped). `:id` takes precedence over `#+NAME:`, and a later attribute
replaces an earlier one with the same key, even from another `#+ATTR_HTML:`.

```````````````````````````````` example
#+NAME: name
#+ATTR_HTML: ignored :id own :title "x & y" :hidden :lang nil
#+ATTR_HTML: :class a :class b
Text.
.
<p class="b" id="own" title="&quot;x &amp; y&quot;">Text.</p>
````````````````````````````````

Like the other affiliated keywords, they belong only to the element directly
following them, even if it is not exported.

```````````````````````````````` example
#+ATTR_HTML: :class c

Not affiliated.

#+ATTR_HTML: :class c
#+begin_comment
x
#+end_comment
Nor this.
.
<p>Not affiliated.</p>
<p>Nor this.</p>
````````````````````````````````

With `ORG_HTML_FLAG_IN_BUFFER_OPTIONS` (the default for `org2html`), the
renderer honors some of the `#+OPTIONS:`.

```````````````````````````````` example
#+OPTIONS: ^:{} todo:nil pri:nil tags:nil -:nil e:nil \n:t
* TODO [#A] Head :tag:
a_b H_{2}O -- \alpha
next
.
<h1>Head</h1>
<p>a_b H<sub>2</sub>O -- \alpha<br>
next</p>
````````````````````````````````

```````````````````````````````` example
#+OPTIONS: f:nil d:nil
Foo[fn:1]
:DRAWER:
hidden
:END:

[fn:1] Foot.
.
<p>Foo</p>
````````````````````````````````

```````````````````````````````` example
#+OPTIONS: ^:nil
a_b
.
<p>a_b</p>
.
--fsubsuperscripts
````````````````````````````````

```````````````````````````````` example
#+OPTIONS: todo:nil
* TODO Head
.
<h1><span class="todo TODO">TODO</span> Head</h1>
.
--fignore-options
````````````````````````````````
